## How You'll Use It Day-to-Day

### Morning Routine (5 min)
1. Open your **Trainer Dashboard** (bookmark it)
2. Check today's schedule — see who's coming in
3. Review any alerts (missed sessions, overdue payments, expiring programs)

### Before Each Session
1. Open the client's profile
2. Review their program — it's already loaded for them to see too
3. After the session, update any notes or metrics

### Weekly (10 min)
1. Update client metrics (weight, body fat, etc.) — clients will see these in their progress tab
2. Review adherence scores
3. Adjust meal plans or programming as needed
4. Check payments — Stripe handles collection automatically, you just monitor

### When Adding a New Client
1. Click **+ Add Client** in the dashboard
2. Fill in their details
3. Choose their plan — Stripe automatically starts billing
4. They get an email with their login link
5. Upload their meal plan and program

## What Your Clients See

When a client opens the app on their phone:

4. **Progress** — Weight, body fat, strength PRs, recovery scores, progress photos
5. **Account** — Membership details, payment management, direct message to you

They can also:

- Book virtual sessions when traveling
- Message you directly through the app
